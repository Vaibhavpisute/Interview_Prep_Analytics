Job title: Data Engineer (made-up sample job description)

About the role
We are a growing analytics team that builds reliable data pipelines for finance and product reporting.
You will design, build and run batch and streaming pipelines, keep our warehouse trustworthy and
work closely with analysts and data scientists.

What you will do
- Build ETL / ELT pipelines in Python and SQL, load them into PostgreSQL and a cloud warehouse.
- Process large datasets with Apache Spark and tune slow jobs (partitioning, joins, skew).
- Design star-schema data models with clear fact and dimension tables.
- Add data quality checks, monitoring and alerts, and fix pipeline failures quickly.
- Automate reports and write clear documentation.

What we look for
- 1-3 years of experience as a Data Engineer or similar role.
- Strong SQL (window functions, query tuning) and Python (pandas, testing).
- Experience with Spark, orchestration tools such as Airflow, and Git / CI.
- Good communication: you can explain trade-offs and work with stakeholders.
- Nice to have: R, dbt, Docker.
